# CrossBorder Marketplace

## Overview

CrossBorder Marketplace is a peer-to-peer marketplace that connects international travelers with buyers who want to purchase products that are significantly cheaper in another country.

Instead of importing products through traditional e-commerce channels, travelers who are already visiting another country can purchase items on behalf of buyers and bring them back legally within applicable customs regulations.

The platform acts as the trusted intermediary by matching buyers and travelers, handling payments through escrow, verifying identities, facilitating communication, and ensuring transparency throughout the transaction.

---

# Problem

Many products are considerably cheaper in certain countries because of:

- Lower taxes
- Regional pricing
- Exchange rates
- Promotions
- Manufacturer pricing differences

For example:

| Product | India | USA |
|---------|-------|------|
| Laptop | ₹2,00,000 | ₹1,50,000 |
| Camera | ₹5,00,000 | ₹4,00,000 |

Currently, people only benefit from these price differences if they have relatives or friends traveling internationally.

Most buyers have no trusted way to connect with travelers.

---

# Solution

Create a marketplace where:

- Travelers register upcoming international trips.
- Buyers post product requests.
- The platform intelligently matches both parties.
- Travelers earn money for carrying products.
- Buyers save money compared to local retail prices.
- The platform earns a service fee.

Everyone benefits.

# Marketplace Model

This is **not** a traditional e-commerce platform.

The platform does not own inventory.

Instead, it connects:

Buyer ↔ Traveler

similar to how:

- Airbnb connects hosts and guests
- Uber connects riders and drivers
- Fiverr connects freelancers and clients

CrossBorder Marketplace connects buyers and international travelers.

---

# Core Workflow

1. Traveler registers an upcoming trip.
2. Buyer creates a product request.
3. Platform matches both users.
4. Traveler accepts the request.
5. Buyer deposits payment into escrow.
6. Traveler purchases the item.
7. Traveler carries the item legally.
8. Buyer receives the product.
9. Buyer confirms delivery.
10. Escrow releases payment.
11. Both users leave ratings.

# Trust & Safety

The platform should include:

- Identity Verification (KYC)
- Passport Verification
- Traveler Reputation
- Buyer Reputation
- Escrow Payments
- Fraud Detection
- Dispute Resolution
- OTP Delivery Verification
- Secure Messaging

---

# Legal & Compliance

The platform must operate within the laws of every supported country.

It should:

- Never encourage customs fraud.
- Never encourage tax evasion.
- Respect import limits.
- Display estimated customs duties where applicable.
- Inform users about prohibited or restricted products.
- Support only legal personal imports.

The traveler is responsible for complying with customs regulations while traveling.
